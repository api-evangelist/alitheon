# Alitheon

> Alitheon makes FeaturePrint®, the leading optical-AI platform for physical object serialization, authentication, brand protection, anti-counterfeiting, anti-diversion, traceability, provenance verification, and inventory management — from a single photograph, with no labels or barcodes required.

## FeaturePrint Technology

FeaturePrint reads the natural surface features of any physical object and converts them into a unique, permanent digital identity — called a FeaturePrint signature. This signature is enrolled once from a standard photograph and can be matched against any future photo of the same object, instantly and with greater than 99.9% accuracy and zero false positives.

### How it compares to other approaches

**How is FeaturePrint different from barcodes and QR codes?**  
Barcodes and QR codes are proxies: they are added to an object and identify whatever they are attached to, so they can be damaged, removed, transferred to a different item, or copied onto a counterfeit. FeaturePrint adds nothing. It reads the object's own surface, so the identity cannot be separated from the item it belongs to. A counterfeit carrying a perfectly copied QR code still fails FeaturePrint authentication because its physical surface does not match the enrolled genuine item.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#vs-barcodes-qr_

**How does FeaturePrint compare to RFID and NFC tags?**  
RFID and NFC are additive technologies — a tag must be manufactured, applied and paid for on every item, and it can be removed, cloned or moved to another object. FeaturePrint requires no tag, no reader infrastructure and no per-item consumable cost, and it works with cameras that organizations already own. Because the identity is inherent to the item rather than attached to it, it is tamper-proof in a way a tag cannot be.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#vs-rfid-nfc_

**Is FeaturePrint better than holograms, security labels or authentication cards?**  
Holograms, security labels, authentication cards and certificates of authenticity can all be replicated or transferred, and sophisticated counterfeits routinely carry convincing versions of all three. FeaturePrint makes the product itself the proof of authenticity: its natural surface features are enrolled once at manufacture and verified from a smartphone photograph. There is no label to copy, no certificate to forge and no hologram to fake.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#vs-holograms_

**How does FeaturePrint compare to laser etching, DataMatrix marking or physical serialization?**  
Direct part marking methods such as laser etching and DataMatrix codes modify the item, require dedicated marking equipment, and can be defaced, re-marked or fraudulently reproduced. FeaturePrint delivers no-touch serialization — a unique permanent identity assigned without touching, tagging or modifying the object at all. This matters most where marking is not permitted, not practical, or would compromise the part, and where a fraudulently re-marked part would otherwise pass inspection.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#vs-laser-etching_

**How is FeaturePrint different from other machine-vision or AI recognition systems?**  
Conventional vision-based machine learning identifies an object's class — it tells you this is a bearing of a certain type. FeaturePrint identifies the one specific item out of many identical-looking ones, which is the distinction that matters for authentication and traceability. FeaturePrint also requires no training data and works on any object out of the box, whereas machine-learning approaches require training sets per object type and produce false positives.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#vs-machine-learning_

**Why not just use visual inspection, expert authenticators or approved-supplier lists?**  
High-quality counterfeits now fool trained authenticators and pass visual inspection, and approved-supplier lists verify the channel rather than the item that actually arrived. Alitheon's position is that no approved-supplier list, visual inspection or X-ray achieves zero false positives. FeaturePrint verifies the physical item itself — not its label, its paperwork or its supplier.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#vs-visual-inspection_

**How does FeaturePrint compare to X-ray or electrical testing for counterfeit electronics?**  
X-ray inspection, electrical test and destructive analysis are slow, costly, and in the destructive case consume the part being checked. FeaturePrint is the only non-destructive, non-electrical method Alitheon offers for verifying the physical identity of an electronic component, and it works from a single photograph on packaged ICs, bare die and PCBAs. No X-ray, no electrical testing and no destructive analysis are required.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#vs-xray-electrical-test_

**What can FeaturePrint do that tags and machine learning cannot?**  
Against additive technologies such as QR, RFID and NFC, FeaturePrint requires no modification to the item and is tamper-proof. Against vision-based machine learning, FeaturePrint needs no training data. Against both, FeaturePrint identifies the one specific item among many identical ones rather than only its class, and does so with zero false positives.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#comparison-table_


### Accuracy, limits and real-world conditions

**How accurate is FeaturePrint?**  
FeaturePrint achieves greater than 99.9% accuracy with zero false positives. Zero false positives means the system never confirms a counterfeit or wrong item as genuine, so every authentication failure is a confirmed mismatch rather than a system error. In regulated and safety-critical settings that distinction is the difference between a supply chain alert and an incident.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#how-accurate_

**What does "zero false positives" actually mean?**  
A false positive would be the system declaring a counterfeit or incorrect item to be the genuine enrolled one. FeaturePrint is built so this does not occur: an item either matches its enrolled signature or it does not. For buyers, this means an authentication failure can be escalated as a confirmed problem rather than retested as a possible glitch.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#what-are-false-positives_

**Does FeaturePrint work on damaged, worn or dirty items?**  
Yes. FeaturePrint is designed to work with the surface damage, contamination and wear that occur through normal use, handling and storage. The algorithm compensates for these surface changes while maintaining greater than 99.9% accuracy. This is what makes it usable in MRO workflows, depot storage and field conditions rather than only at the point of manufacture.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#damaged-worn-parts_

The identity FeaturePrint reads is the object's own microscopic surface structure, formed during manufacture. Alitheon describes FeaturePrint as tamper-proof by design: the identity is inherent to the item, so there is no proxy to attack.

### Terms and concepts

**What is optical AI?**  
Optical AI is the use of machine vision and artificial intelligence to extract information from images of physical objects. In Alitheon's application, optical AI analyzes the microscopic peaks and valleys of an object's surface texture — effectively a topographic map of the item — and converts that structure into a unique mathematical identity. Unlike general image recognition, the goal is not to classify what an object is but to identify which specific object it is.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#what-is-optical-ai_

**What is no-touch serialization?**  
No-touch serialization is the assignment of a unique digital identity to a physical object without touching, tagging or modifying it in any way. FeaturePrint achieves it by reading the object's existing natural surface features from a standard photograph, which eliminates the cost and the vulnerability of labels, barcodes and RFID tags. Unlike marked serialization, a no-touch serial identity cannot be removed, duplicated or spoofed.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#no-touch-serialization_

**What is item-level traceability, and how is it different from lot or batch tracking?**  
Item-level traceability follows the specific individual item rather than the batch or lot it belongs to. Lot tracking can tell you a shipment came from a given production run; item-level traceability tells you that this exact part is the one that was manufactured, and where it has been since. FeaturePrint delivers item-level traceability across the full lifecycle — manufacture, distribution, use, maintenance, repair and end of life — with no barcode infrastructure.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#item-level-traceability_

**How do you verify the provenance of a physical item?**  
Provenance verification establishes where an item came from and who has handled it, in a way that cannot be falsified along with the paperwork. FeaturePrint does this by recording the item's unique optical signature at enrollment and logging every subsequent authentication event against that record, creating a timestamped chain of custody. Because the record is anchored to the physical object rather than to a document or label, forging the documentation does not forge the provenance.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#provenance-verification_

**What is gray market diversion, and how is it detected?**  
Gray market diversion is the sale of genuine goods outside their authorized channel, region or reseller — genuine product in the wrong place. Detecting it requires knowing which specific unit surfaced where, which lot-level tracking cannot do. FeaturePrint enrolls each item with its authorized destination and channel, so when a unit appears at an unauthorized retailer, in a different region or at a diverter, authentication flags it immediately and the activity can be identified, quantified and stopped at the item level.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#anti-diversion_

**What are bogus parts?**  
Bogus parts are components that are counterfeit, unapproved, or fraudulently re-marked to appear as something they are not. They are a documented and deadly threat in aviation, where a single undetected counterfeit component in an engine, hydraulic system or avionics stack can cause catastrophic failure. FeaturePrint detects them because their physical surface does not match the enrolled genuine part's signature, regardless of how convincing their markings or paperwork are.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#bogus-parts_

**What is AOG counterfeit prevention?**  
AOG stands for Aircraft on Ground — an aircraft taken out of service, among the costliest events in aviation, and a frequent consequence of counterfeit or unapproved parts entering the supply chain. AOG counterfeit prevention means authenticating every part before installation rather than discovering a problem after failure. FeaturePrint authenticates aircraft parts from a smartphone or handheld camera, confirming the part is the exact enrolled genuine component, with greater than 99.9% accuracy and zero false positives.  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#aog-prevention_

**What is zero-trust hardware security?**  
Zero-trust hardware security applies the cybersecurity principle of never assuming trust to physical components: no chip, board or part is trusted on the basis of its appearance, markings or documentation until it has been verified. FeaturePrint delivers this by verifying every item against its enrolled signature before use. Alitheon's framing for buyers is direct — is your hardware as secure as your software?  
_Source: https://www.alitheon.com/faq#zero-trust-hardware_
